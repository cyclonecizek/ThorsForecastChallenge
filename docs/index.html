Bias, persistence, TAF and scoring assumptions

Scoring assumptions (confirm with A39T)

Hourly verification: all METAR/SPECI reports within each hour. Wind speed is the highest speed or gust reported, direction is from that report (VRB scores 0), visibility and ceiling are the lowest, present weather is any category reported (vicinity ignored), temperature and dew point come from the routine METAR. The scoring chart doesn't say how a blank Present Wx or "no ceiling" is graded, so those are settings here; the optimizer adapts to whatever you set.
